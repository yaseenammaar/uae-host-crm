How the numbers are worked out
  • Unavailable counts nights from today that Airbnb’s public calendar shows as blocked, over the next 14, 30 and 90 nights. Blocked nights include real bookings, owner blocks, gaps shorter than the minimum stay and dates past the booking window, so this is an upper limit on occupancy, not the occupancy itself.
  • All-in per night is the total Airbnb showed for one sample stay (the first open check-in in the next 90 days, for the minimum stay or 2 nights), fees included, divided by the nights. It runs higher than the nightly rate.
  • Open-night value is open nights × that all-in price. It is an illustration of what the open nights would earn at that price, not lost revenue.
  • Cards are split between Instinct and Claude so nobody checks the same listing twice. Results can be added with “Import results CSV”, using the same columns as the export; rows are matched by Airbnb listing id.
