How editing, enrichment and history work

Only signed-in accounts on the CRM's allow-list can see or change this list. Put your name in “Editing as” so History shows who made each change; deleted prospects can be restored from History.

Enrichers fill email, phone, website, LinkedIn, unit count and notes, then set the enrichment status to Enriched or Needs review, with sources in “Sources and findings” and their name in “Enriched by”.
