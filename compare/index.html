“Active” leaves out listings blocked for all 90 nights, which are most likely paused. Money uses each listing’s sample all-in price and leaves out listings priced over 10,000 AED a night. The largest number in each row is highlighted.

How the numbers are worked out
  • Unavailable counts nights Airbnb’s public calendar shows as blocked from the audit date. Blocked nights include bookings, owner blocks and gaps shorter than the minimum stay, so these are upper limits on occupancy.
  • Booked value is unavailable nights × the listing’s all-in price per night; open-night value is open nights × the same price. Both are illustrations at the sample price, not actual revenue.
  • Bookings from filling 20% is 20% of the open-night value. Operator’s extra commission assumes Airbnb fees and taxes take about 25% of the guest total and the operator keeps 20% of the owner payout, so about 15% of bookings.
  • Each audit is dated by when its calendars were read; audits from different days aren’t measuring exactly the same nights.
